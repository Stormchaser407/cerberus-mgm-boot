## Main Concept Prompt

A cel-shaded cartoon noir parody of a classic old movie studio lion intro, but with Cerberus, a three-headed mythic guardian dog, centered inside a circular golden studio crest. One head is alert and watchful, one head is roaring dramatically, and one head is smoking a cigar with a tired, amused expression. High contrast black background, crimson and violet accents, cinematic emblem design, old film title-card energy, funny but badass, suitable for a vertical phone boot animation.

## Cleaner Emblem Prompt

Three-headed Cerberus guardian dog inside a circular vintage studio emblem, graphic novel style, clean readable silhouette, black background, bone white highlights, subtle crimson glow, one head roaring, one head alert, one head with cigar, mythic security mascot, cinematic boot logo composition.

## Frame Loop Prompt

Animated boot sequence frame concept: Cerberus in a circular crest, center head roaring, left head scanning, right head cigar ember glowing, subtle smoke curl, ring pulsing, dark background, readable phone boot animation, cel-shaded, noir cartoon style.

## Negative Direction

Avoid:

- photorealistic gore
- horror monster excess
- generic cyberpunk robot dog
- overly detailed unreadable frame clutter
- direct MGM logo copying
- copyrighted exact logo elements
